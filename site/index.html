Heads or Hexes

Build the world one map card at a time, walk your party through the land you made, and settle every fight with a flip of the coin.

See the world How it grew

What you do

1

Place the map

Every map card you play becomes land. Forests merge into woods, fields into patchwork farmland, rivers into lakes and houses into towns. The map never ends, and a placed card stays for good.

2

Grow regions

Grow a cluster to its goal and it transforms: your first big forest becomes deep, old forest, later ones fey wood or dark wood. Each finished region raises a landmark, like a heartwood's Tree of Life.

3

Heads, not dice

Checks and attacks flip coins. Your score is how many coins you flip, the target's defence is how many heads you need, and cover, night and blessings add or take away coins you can see.

4

Fight on hexes

Fights zoom into the hex where they start. Your whole party acts, then the foes. Every action is a card on a 7-slot hotbar paid for in action points: no cooldowns, spell slots or mana.

5

Day and night

The day clock moves as you build and travel. The pastel sky drifts from peach at dawn to rose at dusk, and after dark you can only build where your party can see.

6

Play together

Up to four players drop in and out of the host's world with a 6-letter code, each bringing their own hero, on PC and mobile with cross-play.
