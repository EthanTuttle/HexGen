Heads or Hexes

A card-driven hex-crawl RPG: place the map, lead your party across it, and win your fights on the flip of a coin.

See the world How it grew

What you do

1

Place the map

Play map cards to build an endless world, one hex at a time. Woods, farms, rivers and towns blend together as they grow.

2

Grow regions

Grow clusters of one terrain and they transform into special regions, each with its own landmark.

3

Heads, not dice

No dice here: attacks and checks flip coins. The more skilled you are, the more coins you flip.

4

Fight on hexes

When a fight breaks out, the camera drops into that hex and the land becomes a hand-built battlefield. Cover, high ground and choke points all matter.

5

Day and night

Every card and every step moves the clock. Watch the sky turn from peach to rose, and plan for night, when the map closes in around your party.

6

Play together

Bring up to three friends into your world. They join with a short code, bring their own hero, and can leave any time.
