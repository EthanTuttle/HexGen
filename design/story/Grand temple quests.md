# Grand temple quests

The quest each god's grand temple grants (moved from World & story, Nov 21, 2026). Rules (the temple is its god's holy land, a tier 3 region; services; rewards set per god): [World & story](../01%20World%20%26%20story.md) → Grand temple quests.

## Aurora (sun)

Relight sun shrines at dawn across several hexes.

## Selene (moon)

Follow a moonlit path that only appears at night.

## Morin (forge)

Reforge Morin's broken hammer from scattered pieces.

## Toyoko (bounty and harvests)

Cleanse corrupted fields blighting the land.

## Adam (earth)

Calm an earthquake-causing spirit deep underground.

## Canto (music)

Collect lost verses of Canto's hymn across towns.

## Eve (trickery)

New quest needed (the moving-temple quest was dropped Oct 24, 2026)

## Tod (death)

Survive a journey through the land of the dead.
