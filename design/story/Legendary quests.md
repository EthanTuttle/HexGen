# Legendary quests

Each tier 3 region's shortcut quest: where it starts and how it plays out (moved from World & story, Nov 21, 2026). Shortcut quests are also where lore threads get tied in (Ethan, Oct 24, 2026). Rules (every tier 3 gets one, once per campaign; how the guarantee works): [World & story](../01%20World%20%26%20story.md) → Other Legendary quests.

## Hoboken

**Region:** Great city (village). **Starts:** A merchant at a Trading House (market town) offers a merchant charter.

Escort a caravan along a dangerous route; the quest places towns on the map to bring the merchant to. Completing it guarantees the great city.

## The Circus

**Region:** Fairgrounds (plains). **Starts:** A ringmaster's lost animal shows up at a Farm landmark.

Return the animal; this guarantees the fairgrounds.

## Legendary dwarf forge

**Region:** Dwarven hold (hills). **Starts:** A smith at a highlands Clan Hall tells of a lost forge.

Gather rare ore from a Legendary dungeon (a step the quest places), then relight the forge (rewards an epic forged weapon); guarantees the dwarven hold.

## Ancient library

**Region:** Buried city (desert). **Starts:** The Sandstone Sphinx (dunes) tells of the buried city as a riddle reward.

Dig it out (steps: open).

## Named castles

**Region:** Occupied realm (hills, plains or village). **Starts:** Shortcut: a dispossessed noble at a fortified town's Keep asks for help (shared pattern for every castle).

Rally allies, then assault the castle; retaking it frees the region. Who holds it varies per castle.

## Grand temples (8)

**Region:** Holy lands in each god's terrain. **Starts:** Shortcut: a pilgrimage from a priest at a Church landmark (shared pattern for every god); each temple then grants its own quest.

Each temple's own quest.

## Sword in the Stone

**Region:** Placed by the Lady of the Lake's quest (enchanted lake, river/lake). **Starts:** Meeting Lady of the Lake. Enchanted lake shortcut: a bard at an Inn landmark sings of the Lady.

Scenes: [The Lady of the Lake](../scenes/lady-of-the-lake.json), [The Lady of the Lake: beaten](../scenes/lady-of-the-lake-beaten.json), [The Lady of the Lake: the blade returns](../scenes/lady-of-the-lake-blade-returns.json) and [The sword pull](../scenes/sword-pull.json). The Lady: [Lady of the Lake](../characters/Lady%20of%20the%20Lake.md).

1. A river/lake region becomes an enchanted lake (tier 3), whose location is Lady of the Lake. Meeting her starts the quest.
2. A social encounter with the water maiden: she tells the player where the sword is, and the quest places Sword in the Stone on the map.
3. Optionally, the player can win the Lady's blessing (ways to win it: the scene [The Lady of the Lake](../scenes/lady-of-the-lake.json)). Pulling the sword out is a STR coin check; the blessing adds coins to it. Without the blessing, the player can still try with brute force.
4. Reaching Sword in the Stone and pulling the sword out completes the quest. A failed pull locks the sword out for good, adding stakes and repercussions to the choice of whether to seek the blessing first. The pull plays out in the same zoomed-hex scene as the talk with the Lady.

**Her champion and the deed** (decided Oct 28, 2026): Sir Bedivere was her sworn knight, who guarded the lake for her and rode out to face something he couldn't beat; she mourns him. He fell far from the lake, in a chimera's lair, which the quest places on the map far away (matching her line "He fell far from these waters"). The chimera still dens there with his blade. She never says his name: the player learns it from the blade they carry back, the item "Sir Bedivere's Blade". (In the legend, Bedivere is the knight who returned Excalibur to the Lady.)

**The old kings** (decided Oct 28, 2026: left vague): a legend only. Their names are cut in the stone, but they have no set history.

## Dragon mountain

**Region:** Dragon mountain (hills); location: Redbone's Lair. **Starts:** Cultists court Redbone, the fire-dragon king, for Egan; find him first (ties to the main story).

## Fey Gate

**Region:** Feywild crossing (forest). **Starts:** As the rift grows it thins the veil; a fey creature slips through and begs you to open a way home (ties to the main story).

## Royal Granary

**Region:** Breadbasket kingdom (farmland). **Starts:** A priest of Toyoko at a Great Windmill wants to raise a realm of plenty.
