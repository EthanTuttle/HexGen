# Noble path

Merchant lines have been disrupted by corrupted hexes; sent as an envoy to investigate. The investigation leads to a traveling merchant Story card. These corrupted hexes are a special early case, appearing before the rift: the story places them at fixed locations.

Mentor: [The noble's parent](../characters/The%20noble%27s%20parent.md). Companions: [Leo Conley](../characters/Leo%20Conley.md) and [Reginald](../characters/Reginald.md).

## Route to Gronogon

Investigates corrupted hexes disrupting merchant lines → traveling merchant Story card → instructed to go to Gronogon to alert the nobility to a rise in curses, and is roped into the main plot there
