# Criminal path

Steals an artifact important to the cast-out god: a key to the god's prison.

Mentor: [The client](../characters/The%20client.md). Companions: [Dolmund](../characters/Dolmund.md) and [Lilian](../characters/Lilian.md).

## Route to Gronogon

Steals the key → the client consumes it, opening the rift, and teleports away → the hero is arrested and taken to Gronogon, where they are offered freedom if they track down the cultists and stop the plot
