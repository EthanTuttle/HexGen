# Folk Hero path

The innkeeper asks them to save his pet cat, snatched by cultists who sensed fey magic they could use; the cat becomes one of the path's 2 companions (a special companion-only race with its own trait and racial card, like other races: see [Classes & combat](../03%20Classes%20%26%20combat.md) → Races; wizard class).

Mentor: [The innkeeper](../characters/The%20innkeeper.md). Companions: [Fluffy](../characters/Fluffy.md) and [Chelmsford](../characters/Chelmsford.md).

## Route to Gronogon

Rescues the cat → cultists kill the innkeeper → goes to Gronogon to learn why a banned religion is on the rise
