# Kris Huntington

Acolyte path companion. Bio, home and personal quest: [World & story](../01%20World%20%26%20story.md) → NPC homes and stories. Proficiencies: World & story → Companion proficiencies. How to write: [Writing guide](00%20Writing%20guide.md).

## Voice sheet

Approved by Ethan, Oct 28, 2026. From the bio: a wood golem ranger (Hunter), carved by a druid to guard the forest from beasts, and she's taken it a little far; wears animal pelts; Austrian accent; her mind is always on the next hunt; personal quest: the White Stag. Full bio: [World & story](../01%20World%20%26%20story.md) → NPC homes and stories.

- **How she talks:** blunt and few. Short and plain, never a word wasted; she says exactly what she means, and the humour comes from how literal she is.
- **Accent:** no contractions ("I do not", "it is") as the base, with the odd German word ("Ja", "Gut"), used sparingly. "Ja" is seasoning, never the voice (Writing guide → Tells to avoid).
- **Never:** runs from a beast, however big; uses contractions.
- **What she wants and what worries her:** wants the next hunt, whatever is bigger than the last thing. Worries about getting soft, or the hunt ending.
- **Sounds like:** Arnold Schwarzenegger: Austrian, blunt, deadpan, oddly lovable.
- **When she's not joking:** forest-old. Slow and ancient, like the wood she was carved from; she talks about the druid and the trees.
- **Sample lines** (drafted by Claude, picked by Ethan, Oct 28, 2026):
  - "We stop here. The wind is wrong. Everything in that wood can smell us now."
  - "The druid made me to keep the beasts out. Nobody told me when to stop."
  - "I do not sleep. I stand in the rain and grow a little. It is nice."
  - "That is not a monster, it is a goat. We hunt it anyway, for practice."
