# Places

What gets said in generated places: towns now, and later landmarks, camps, road merchants and so on (set up Oct 30, 2026). Places are rolled, not hand-built, so their lines are written per **moment slot** and per place type rather than per place. How to write: [Writing guide](00%20Writing%20guide.md). How towns show: [Presentation](../07%20Presentation.md) → Town screen.

## How slots work

Decided with Ethan, Oct 30, 2026.

- **A slot is a moment that needs a line**, such as walking into a town or opening the shop. Each place type fills its own slots.
- **3 lines per slot per place type**, picked at random, so towns repeat less.
- **Fill-ins** go in brackets and are swapped in by the game, e.g. [Town name], [item], [price].
- **Day and night:** only the arrive beat has a day and a night version (3 of each). Other slots are the same at any time.
- **Speakers:** the arrive beat is narration (Writing guide → Dialogue rules: second person, says what the party sees, never what the hero thinks). Keepers speak their own slots.
- **Keepers are generic by type**, with no names: "the trader", "the innkeeper". A slum's keeper and a Keep's keeper sound different, but every slum's keeper sounds the same.
- **A new kind of place** (a landmark, a camp) gets its own section here, with its slots listed first, then its lines per type.

## Towns

**Town names** (decided Oct 30, 2026): built from parts that lean on the town type (e.g. fortified: "-hold", market: "-market", slums: "-end"). The player can still rename a town ([Map & exploration](../02%20Map%20%26%20exploration.md) → Exploration and terrain). The parts lists: not written yet.

**Slots** (decided Oct 30, 2026)

| Slot | Speaker | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrive | Narration | The party walks into the town square (replaces [Town greeting]); a day and a night version |
| Shop: open | Shopkeeper | The shop's counter opens |
| Shop: buy | Shopkeeper | An item is bought |
| Shop: sell | Shopkeeper | An item is sold |
| Shop: can't afford | Shopkeeper | The party is short of gold |
| Shop: leave | Shopkeeper | Back to the square |
| Inn: open | Innkeeper | The inn opens |
| Inn: rest | Innkeeper | Rest the night is picked, before the night at the bar |
| Inn: can't afford | Innkeeper | The party is short of gold |
| Inn: morning | Innkeeper | With the rest results |
| Quest board: open | Narration or a keeper (open) | The board opens |
| Quest note | Written on the note | Each quest: its giver, name and goal |

**Town types** (from [Map & exploration](../02%20Map%20%26%20exploration.md) → Region variants and locations): market town, Trading House, fortified town, Keep, slums, Black Market, scholars' town, College, Great city and named cities, and inns. Their lines: not written yet.
