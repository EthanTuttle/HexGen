Soldier path companion. Bio, home and personal quest: [World & story](../01%20World%20%26%20story.md) → NPC homes and stories. Proficiencies: World & story → Companion proficiencies. How to write: [Writing guide](00%20Writing%20guide.md).

## Voice sheet

Approved by Ethan, Oct 28, 2026. From the bio: a human bard (Narrator), enraptured by the hero, who follows them to sing their praises; a failed bard who needs one great hero's tale to make his name; plays clan feasts at the Clan Hall. Full bio: [World & story](../01%20World%20%26%20story.md) → NPC homes and stories.

- **Why he's failed so far:** too much. He over-embellishes: every tale grows ten verses and three dragons. (He can rhyme; that isn't the problem.)
- **How he talks:** always performing. Longer, flowing sentences that fit a narrator bard; every sentence is a little performance, even "pass the bread", building to an image or a flourish.
- **Never:** tells anything plain (he can't describe something without adding to it), or mocks the hero (he's enraptured).
- **What he wants and what worries him:** wants to be there for every moment of the hero's legend. Worries about missing the big moment, or the hero dying before the ending.
- **Sounds like:** a sports commentator: breathless, live play-by-play of every swing. (Chosen over Gilderoy Lockhart so the Soldier path doesn't have two dishonest characters; his embellishing is hype, not lies, and Mel keeps the lying.)
- **When he's not joking:** lost for words. For once he can't find a line: short and halting, and that's how you know.
- **Sample lines** (drafted by Claude, picked by Ethan, Oct 28, 2026):
  - "Hold still a moment. I want to remember exactly how you looked just then."
  - "Every bard gets one great story. I think you're mine."
  - "Could you do that again, only slower, and facing the light?"
  - "Verse twelve is where you fight the dragon. We haven't fought a dragon yet, but I'm hopeful."
  - "Lovely swordwork. Could you shout something heroic next time? 'Have at thee', anything."
