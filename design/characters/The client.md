# The client

How to write: [Writing guide](00%20Writing%20guide.md).

## Profile

- **Role:** Mentor, Villain
- **Path:** Criminal
- **Status:** Voice sheet not started

## Background

Hired the hero to steal the artifact, a key to the cast-out god's prison. Exits by consuming the artifact, which opens the rift, then teleporting away. Later returns as the avatar of the force.

**Mentor details:** The cult's high priest. Like a cleric's bond, he is helping Egan the most, so Egan grants him the most power, which is what makes him the avatar.
