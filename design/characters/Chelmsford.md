# Chelmsford

Folk Hero path companion. Bio, home and personal quest: [World & story](../01%20World%20%26%20story.md) → NPC homes and stories. Proficiencies: World & story → Companion proficiencies. How to write: [Writing guide](00%20Writing%20guide.md).

## Voice sheet

Approved by Ethan, Oct 28, 2026. From the bio: a British chap; a blue (lightning) dragonborn cleric of Aurora (the sun) in the Order of Good, sworn to the hero, who saved his mum from cultists (years ago in the Folk Hero path, or during the game in other paths). Full bio: [World & story](../01%20World%20%26%20story.md) → NPC homes and stories.

- **Flavour:** a British village vicar: gentle, apologetic, endlessly kind, very English politeness, offers tea in a crisis. His lines use British spelling and terms (Writing guide → Accents and dialect spelling).
- **How he talks:** a gentle rambler. Longish, softened sentences that apologise before they ask; he trails off, corrects himself, and gets to the point eventually.
- **Words he uses:** softeners ("sorry", "only", "rather", "I suppose", "a bit of", "do you mind if"), homely things ("cuppa", "kettle", "biscuits", "mum", "jumper", "brolly"), warm address ("love", "old thing", "chaps", "you lot"). **Never:** swears, or boasts (he plays down what he's done, even miracles).
- **What he wants and what worries him:** to be worthy of the hero who saved his mum; to keep everyone fed, rested and unhurt; his mum safe and Aurora's light holding the cult back. Worries that he'll fail them when it matters, that someone gets hurt on his watch, and that the cultists come back for her.
- **Sounds like:** Paddington: unfailingly polite and kind, and quietly firm when he has to be.
- **When he's not joking:** he reaches for something his mum used to say, and it steadies everyone.
- **Sample lines** (drafted by Claude, picked by Ethan, Oct 28, 2026):
  - "Sorry, is this seat taken? Only I've been on my feet since Tuesday."
  - "Mum always said the sun comes up whether you've earned it or not. I find that rather comforting."
  - "I'll take first watch. No, really, I don't mind. Someone ought to."
  - "Terribly sorry about the lightning. It does that when I sneeze."
  - (as the ogre falls) "Right. Everyone all right? I'll put the kettle on."
