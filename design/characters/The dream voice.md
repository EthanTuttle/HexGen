# The dream voice

How to write: [Writing guide](00%20Writing%20guide.md).

## Profile

- **Role:** Mentor
- **Path:** Acolyte
- **Status:** Voice sheet not started

## Background

The Acolyte's own god, chosen by the player. Appears in the hero's dreams and speaks in their mind, telling them to follow a trail to a cultist dungeon (a Story card), then to go to Gronogon. There the voice is cut off by the rift and stops appearing; the reason stays unexplained to the player until later.

**Mentor details:** The rift is draining the gods' reach; all eight are weakening, and the Acolyte is the first to notice when their god goes silent at Gronogon. This is story only: cleric and Holy Defender power works normally in play.
