## Background

Revealed as a traitor. While the hero is away tracking the attackers, he lets more cultists into the guard tower and they kill most of the guards. He did not expect the hero to return alive. After killing him, the Soldier travels to Gronogon.

**Mentor details:** A true believer, secretly a cultist all along.
